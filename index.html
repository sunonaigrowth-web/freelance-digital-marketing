Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before enrolling

Who is eligible for these programs? +
Most programs accept any graduate between 21–30 years. Some programs like Paytm Disha even accept 10th/12th pass candidates. Mahindra Finance is for women only, and Finova VMP is for male graduates only. Our counsellor will guide you to the right program.
Is the job guarantee real? What if I don't get placed? +
Yes, 100% real. Every program comes with a written placement commitment. If you complete the full program but don't get placed, you receive a complete refund of your course fee. No questions asked — administered directly by Crack-ED (CarDekho Group).
Can I enroll if I can't pay the full fee upfront? +
Absolutely. We have flexible EMI options from ₹5,000/month and education loans up to ₹3 lakh. There are also free programs like Axis Bank BDA with zero course fee. Contact us on WhatsApp and we'll find the right financial option for you.
Do I need to relocate outside Northeast India? +
No. We focus on placement within Northeast India — Assam, Meghalaya, Manipur, Nagaland, Tripura, Arunachal Pradesh & Sikkim. Some online programs like Axis Bank VRM let you work from your current city.
How long does it take to get a job after enrolling? +
Most students receive their offer letters within 60–90 days. Programs range from 2-week virtual training (Paytm Disha) to 6-month programs (Bandhan Bank AM). Longer programs also pay stipends during training, so you earn while you learn.
What is the salary after getting placed? +
Salaries range from ₹1.8 LPA to ₹5.5 LPA depending on the program, plus monthly incentives on top of fixed CTC. As you grow Sales Exec → RM → Senior RM → Branch Manager, salaries increase significantly. Banking is one of India's best sectors for career growth.
